# CEDA Innovation Platform Hackathon — Execution Plan
*(Window: opens 9 Nov → closes 12 Nov 2026. Compliance checks complete by 14 Nov. Today: 11 Nov.)*

---

## 1. Objective — what is actually being judged

You are not shipping a finished product. Per §4.3, the deliverable is a **technical proposal (with attachments)** that "clearly illustrates how the proposed platform will function" for:
1. **Registration** of participants, and
2. **Submission of solutions** (ideas → review → feedback → approval → awards)

Judging is **rubric-based** (§5.2c) and compliance is checked within 48h of close — so the winning move is: a **complete, paper-tight proposal** whose sections visibly map 1:1 onto the published functional pillars, plus **just enough visual proof** (architecture + key screens) to make the platform feel real.

> Rule of the endgame: **A complete good submission beats an incomplete brilliant one.** Lock completeness first, then polish.

## 2. The six scoring pillars → where to spend effort

| # | Pillar (from §3.2) | Proof to include in the proposal | Effort weight |

| 1 | **Role-based access & IP protection** (d, h) | RBAC matrix (4 roles × permissions), NDA/data-confidentiality flow, "judge sees only what's assigned" story | ★★★★★ |

| 6 | **Scalability / cloud / future** (g) | One clean architecture slide: modular services, cloud-hosted, API-ready (do NOT build ML now) | ★★★ |

## 3. Workstreams (run in this order)

**Track A — Proposal document (MUST-HAVE)**
- Executive summary (what the platform is, in 5 sentences)
- Platform walkthrough: registration flow + submission flow, step by step
- One section **per pillar** above, each opening with the pillar's own words from the guidelines (mirroring their language = rubric-friendly)
- Team section: 2 members × the 4 roles (UX/UI, Developer, Business & Technical Analyst) — state explicitly who wears which two hats and why that covers §5.1
- Compliance section: DPA data-handling statement + eligibility confirmations

**Track B — Visual artifacts (DIFFERENTIATOR)**
- 1 architecture diagram (modular, cloud, API-ready)
- 1 RBAC/permission matrix
- 1 workflow diagram (stage gates + feedback loop)
- 3–5 key screen sketches: submission form, status tracker, judge review view, admin dashboard
- *Reuse the same diagrams in both the doc and the pitch, don't make two versions*

**Track C — Compliance & completeness QA (GATE)**
- Every attachment the brief names is attached (check "documents as per attachment" in §4.3)
- Eligibility evidence ready: age, citizenship, 50% shareholding proof (if company entry)
- Submitted via **CEDA portal** (§4.2) — email fallback only if the portal is down, screenshot the portal error as evidence
- Submit **before** the 12 Nov close — do not ride the deadline; the portal may be slammed by other last-minute entrants
